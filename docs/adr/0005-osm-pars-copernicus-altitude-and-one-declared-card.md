# OpenStreetMap pars, Copernicus altitude, and a declared card that is an exception

ADR 0001 says any new Source needs an ADR. This one adds two that pass the right-to-publish test
ADR 0002 set, and records one fact source that does not, as a decision rather than as a loophole.

## The problem

The Tour's autumn swing keeps landing on Courses OpenGolfAPI does not hold, and a Course built
from a Tournament's own Wikipedia article has a name, a par and a total and nothing else. On
2026-11-04, the day before the World Wide Technology Championship, El Cardonal showed Length and
Par and seven unavailable lines, and the ranking rested on 30% of its Weighting. 12 other stored
Courses were in the same position.

## What was checked

**OpenStreetMap holds pars for some of them.** Its data is ODbL 1.0, the licence OpenGolfAPI is
already under, and `golf=hole` ways carry a hole number and a par. Coverage is partial. Port
Royal, Country Club of Jackson and Hurstbourne have them. Corales and El Cardonal do not.

**OpenStreetMap's lengths are not a card.** A `golf=hole` line is what a mapper drew. Waialae's
lines sum to 7,116 yards against 7,020 published, close enough to pass the 3% sum check that
`holes_trusted` makes, while its first hole measures 525. A longest par 4 read from that would
state the error as a fact, so **we take pars and coordinates from the map, never yardages**.

**The Copernicus DEM gives an altitude anywhere.** GLO-90 is free to use with attribution, and
Open-Meteo serves it without a key. It is one reading on a 90 metre grid, so the page says "about".

**Nothing open holds Slope, Rating or green surface for these Courses.** Those lines stay blank.

## The decision

**OpenStreetMap is a Source, for pars and coordinates, read from a committed snapshot.**
`data/osm-course-holes.json` is regenerated by a person with `npm run snapshot:osm-courses`, for
Courses a person has matched to a map id. It is not a daily stage: Overpass's public server asks
automated users to run their own, and refused most requests the day this was written. The snapshot
is public in this repo under ODbL, which also discharges the share-alike. A Course's own holes from
OpenGolfAPI are never replaced.

**Copernicus is a Source, for altitude only, and Wikipedia is preferred where an article states
one.** Read once per Course, in the daily ingest, and not asked again.

**El Cardonal's card is declared by hand, and that is an exception to ADR 0002.** Jack decided this
on 2026-11-04 with the objection on the table. Its 18 pars and the 18 yardages of its longest tee
are entered in `DECLARED_CARDS` in `lib/courses/cards.ts`, read from GolfPass's listing because the
club's own site offered no scorecard to read.

This does not pass the test in ADR 0002. We have no licence or written grant from GolfPass or from
the club. The argument for it is that a course's par and yardage are facts anyone can read off the
card on the first tee, and that 36 numbers are not a database. That is exactly the judgement call
ADR 0002 says does not satisfy the test, so it is written down here as a judgement call rather than
presented as a right.

Its limits:

- **Named Courses only.** `declared` is not a route for filling gaps generally. Another entry needs
  someone to decide it, and to amend this list.
- **The card and no more.** Pars and yardages. Not GolfPass's rating and Slope, which disagree with
  other listings, nor its descriptions, reviews or layout.
- **Marked on the page.** The site says the card was entered by hand, links where from, and says it
  is not openly licensed.
- **It goes when it can.** An entry is removed once an open Source holds the Course, and removed
  immediately if GolfPass or the club objects.

## Consequences

**The snapshot starts small.** It holds El Cardonal, for its coordinates alone, and Port Royal.
Overpass refused too many requests on the day to match the rest to map ids. Adding one is a line in
`scripts/snapshot-osm-courses.ts` and a re-run.

**A Course with OpenStreetMap pars gets its par mix and par 5 share, and not its par 4 lengths.**
The page gives that reason rather than the older one about Wikipedia.

**A declared card gets the full set of hole Traits**, through the same `holes_trusted` check as any
other: it is summed against the published total, and refused if it disagrees.

**The published total stays Wikipedia's 7,363, the club's longest tee.** A search result citing the
Tour's own figure says the tournament plays longer. That figure is not one we may read.

**The elevation model reads one point.** At the middle of the map's outline, or OpenGolfAPI's
location, which may be the clubhouse. It is about the site, not any hole on it.
